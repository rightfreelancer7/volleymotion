VolleyMotion Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VolleyMotion (the "App"), provided by Choksha Lalam ("we," "us," or "our"), accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

1. Summary

  • VolleyMotion uses the front camera to recognize movements for gameplay.
  • Camera images and video frames are processed on the device and are not intentionally saved by the App or uploaded to a server controlled by us.
  • Gameplay and activity statistics may be calculated and stored locally on your device.
  • VolleyMotion does not require Google Health Connect or Apple HealthKit to calculate its gameplay and activity statistics.
  • We do not operate a VolleyMotion user-account database.
  • Google Play or Apple processes subscriptions and payments made through their respective stores.

2. Camera and Pose Processing

VolleyMotion requests camera permission because camera-controlled gameplay is a core feature of the App.

The front camera is used to detect pose landmarks and movements such as swings, jumps, blocks, hand raises, and tracking readiness. Pose analysis is performed on the device.

VolleyMotion does not intentionally record gameplay video, take or save camera photographs, upload camera frames to a server controlled by us, or use camera images for advertising or identity recognition.

Movement and pose results are used to control gameplay and calculate gameplay and activity statistics.

You may deny or later disable camera permission in your device settings. Camera-controlled gameplay will not function while camera permission is denied.

3. Gameplay and Activity Statistics

VolleyMotion may calculate and display gameplay and activity statistics based on gameplay duration and detected movements.

These statistics may include:

  • active gameplay time;
  • camera-ready tracked or standing time;
  • detected swings and hand raises;
  • estimated jump categories or counts; and
  • other estimated gameplay or activity statistics.

Certain fitness-related estimates displayed by the App are calculated locally using gameplay duration and detected movement.

VolleyMotion does not request your actual weight, age, heart rate, medical records, or Health Connect or HealthKit information for these calculations.

Gameplay and activity values are estimates. Their accuracy may vary because of camera placement, lighting, framing, movement visibility, device performance, and individual physical differences.

These statistics are provided for general gameplay, informational, and fitness purposes and are not guaranteed or medically validated measurements.

4. Local Storage

VolleyMotion may store gameplay and activity-summary information and certain App settings locally on your device.

Locally stored information may include:

  • gameplay time;
  • movement counts and activity statistics;
  • gameplay statistics;
  • onboarding preferences;
  • sound and coaching settings;
  • camera-disclosure acceptance; and
  • other settings necessary to remember your App preferences.

This information is stored locally to provide App functionality, display applicable gameplay or activity statistics, and remember your preferences.

We do not receive these locally stored gameplay or activity statistics through a VolleyMotion-operated user account or backend server.

5. Accounts

VolleyMotion does not require you to create a user account with us.

We do not maintain a VolleyMotion user-account database containing usernames, passwords, profiles, or similar user-account information.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, restoration, and payment processing are handled by Google Play on Android and Apple's App Store on iOS.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, Google Account password, Apple Account password, or bank-account credentials.

VolleyMotion may receive store-provided purchase status or transaction information necessary to provide or restore subscribed access.

Google or Apple handles information under its own privacy policies, account terms, and applicable legal requirements.

You can manage or cancel a subscription through your Google Play or Apple subscription settings. Deleting VolleyMotion does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your camera images, locally stored gameplay statistics, activity statistics, or health-related information.

Camera and pose information used by VolleyMotion is processed for camera-controlled gameplay and is not intentionally uploaded to a server controlled by us.

Information may be processed independently by platform providers and software components necessary to operate the App, including Google Play, Apple's App Store, and on-device pose-processing components, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our legal rights, or the security and integrity of the App.

8. Data Security

VolleyMotion minimizes data handling by processing camera and pose information on the device and storing applicable gameplay and activity summaries locally.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled by the App. However, no electronic processing, device, storage system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and Google or Apple accounts.

9. Data Retention

VolleyMotion does not intentionally store or retain camera images, gameplay video recordings, or camera frames on servers controlled by us. Camera and pose information used for real-time gameplay is processed on the device and is not retained by us after the processing required for the applicable gameplay interaction.

Gameplay statistics, activity statistics, App settings, and preferences that are stored locally may remain on your device until they are overwritten through normal App use, cleared through your device settings, reset through an available App function, or removed when VolleyMotion is uninstalled.

We do not retain these locally stored gameplay and activity statistics on a VolleyMotion-operated server.

Google, Apple, and other independent platform providers may retain purchase, transaction, diagnostic, or account information according to their own privacy policies, retention requirements, and applicable law.

10. Data Deletion and Deletion Requests

VolleyMotion does not require users to create an account, and we do not maintain a VolleyMotion user-account database.

You can delete information stored locally by VolleyMotion by clearing the App's storage or data through your device settings or by uninstalling VolleyMotion.

Because camera images, gameplay video recordings, and camera frames are not intentionally stored on servers controlled by us, there is normally no such server-stored camera information for us to delete.

11. Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians should supervise children using camera-based physical gameplay and ensure that the play area is safe.

12. Health and Medical Disclaimer

VolleyMotion is a fitness-oriented motion game and is not a medical device.

Gameplay and activity statistics provided by VolleyMotion are estimates for general informational and fitness purposes.

VolleyMotion does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition and does not provide medical advice.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Stop physical activity and seek appropriate assistance if you experience pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or other concerning symptoms.
