Volley Motion Game Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Volley Motion Game (the “App”), provided by Choksha Lalam (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

1. Summary

  • The App uses the front camera to recognize movements for gameplay.
  • Camera images are processed on the device and are not intentionally saved by the App or uploaded to our server.
  • Activity totals and estimated calories are stored locally on the device.
  • The App does not use Google Health Connect or Apple HealthKit.
  • We do not operate an App account database or subscription-verification backend.
  • Google Play or Apple processes subscriptions and payments.

2. Camera and pose processing

The App requests camera permission because camera-controlled gameplay is a core feature. The front camera is used to detect pose landmarks and movements such as swings, jumps, blocks, hand raises, and tracking readiness. Pose analysis is performed on the device.

The App does not intentionally record gameplay video, take or save camera photographs, upload camera frames to an App server, or use camera images for advertising or identity recognition. The App uses movement and pose results to control gameplay and calculate estimated activity statistics.

You may deny or later disable camera permission in your device settings. Camera-controlled gameplay will not function while permission is denied.

3. Activity and fitness estimates

The App may calculate and display estimates including:

  • active gameplay time;
  • camera-ready tracked or standing time;
  • detected swings and hand raises;
  • estimated jump categories or counts; and
  • estimated calorie expenditure.

Estimated kcal is calculated locally from active gameplay time and detected movement using a fixed 150 lb (68.0 kg) reference person. The App does not request your actual weight, age, heart rate, medical records, or other Health Connect or HealthKit information for this calculation.

Important: Activity values and calorie expenditure are approximations. They may vary because of camera placement, lighting, framing, movement visibility, device performance, and individual physical differences. They are not guaranteed measurements.

4. Local storage

The App stores activity-summary totals and certain settings locally on your device. These may include gameplay time, movement counts, estimated calories, onboarding preferences, sound settings, and camera-disclosure acceptance. This information is used to display current-day statistics and remember App preferences.

We do not receive these local activity totals through an App-operated account or backend. You can remove them by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or deleting the App from your device. Deleting the App may not cancel an active store subscription.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, restoration, and payment processing are handled by Google Play on Android and Apple on iOS. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, Google password, Apple Account password, or bank-account credentials.

The App receives store-provided purchase status and transaction information necessary to provide or restore subscribed access. Google or Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy and account terms. You can manage or cancel a subscription through your Google Play or Apple subscription settings. Cancellation normally stops future renewals; access may continue through the already-paid period.

6. Information sharing

We do not sell camera images, locally stored activity totals, or health information. Information may be processed by the platform providers and software components needed to operate the App, including Google Play, Apple App Store, and on-device pose-processing components, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security and integrity of the App. Because the present App does not operate a user-account backend, we ordinarily do not possess an App account record identifying individual players.

7. Data security and retention

The App minimizes data handling by processing pose information and saving activity summaries locally. No method of electronic processing or device storage is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and store account.

Local information remains on the device until it is overwritten through normal App use, cleared through device settings, or removed when the App is deleted. Google and Apple apply their own retention rules to purchases and store-account records.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children using camera-based physical gameplay and ensure that the play area is safe. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us using the method below.

9. Health and medical disclaimer

Volley Motion Game is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. The App does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Stop physical activity and seek appropriate help if you experience pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or other concerning symptoms.

10. International use

If you use the App outside the United States, applicable privacy and consumer-protection rights may vary. Google and Apple may process store and transaction information in locations described in their respective privacy notices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its data practices, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or store listing when appropriate.

12. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the developer contact information displayed on the App’s Google Play or Apple App Store listing.
