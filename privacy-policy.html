Last updated: March 20, 2026

VolleyMotion ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, VolleyMotion.

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Camera and motion data: The app may use your device camera to detect body gestures and player movements during gameplay.
  • Device information: Such as device model, operating system, app version, and performance/crash data.
  • Usage data: Such as gameplay interactions, session duration, and feature usage.
  • Advertising data: If ads are shown, third-party advertising providers may collect limited device identifiers and usage information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve gameplay features
  • Enable motion or gesture-based game interaction

  • Show advertisements, where applicable
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Camera Access

VolleyMotion may request access to your camera solely for gameplay and gesture/motion recognition features. Camera data is used in real time to support the app experience.

We do not sell your camera data. We do not intentionally record or store personal video footage unless explicitly stated in a feature you choose to use.

4. Advertising

The app may display third-party advertisements, such as Google AdMob or similar ad providers. These services may collect and use certain information, such as device identifiers, approximate location, and app interaction data, to provide and improve ads.

Please review the privacy policies of those third-party providers for more details.

We may use analytics or crash reporting tools to understand app usage, diagnose issues, and improve performance. These tools may collect non-personal technical information such as:

  • Device type

  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • With service providers that help us operate the app, such as analytics or advertising platforms

7. Children’s Privacy

VolleyMotion is not directed to children under the age of 13 unless stated otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the app, improve services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices

You may be able to limit certain data collection by:
