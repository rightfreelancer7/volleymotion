Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

VolleyMotion ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the VolleyMotion mobile application uses information and device permissions.

1. Camera and Motion Recognition

VolleyMotion uses your device camera to recognize body movements used to control gameplay, such as jumps, arm movements, swings, and blocks.

Camera images are processed for real-time gameplay. VolleyMotion does not intentionally record or save video footage as part of normal gameplay.

You can disable camera access at any time in your device settings. Camera-based gameplay will not function without camera permission.

2. Gameplay and Activity Information

The app may calculate gameplay information such as match results, play duration, detected movements, jumps, and estimated activity or calorie information.

Activity and calorie values are estimates for general gameplay information only. They are not medical or fitness-device measurements and should not be used for medical purposes.

Daily activity summaries used by the current version of the app are stored locally on your device.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

VolleyMotion may offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by the platform store, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card or bank information.

The app may receive purchase status or entitlement information from the platform store so that it can provide or restore purchased features.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of VolleyMotion does not contain advertising or use an advertising SDK. We do not use your camera or gameplay information for advertising.

VolleyMotion does not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.

If advertising or related data practices are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable app-store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

The current app is not intended to use gameplay or camera information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Platform providers and app stores may process technical, purchase, diagnostic, or service information according to their own policies when providing their services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not intentionally share camera footage or locally stored gameplay activity summaries with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay activity summaries stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared by the app, removed with the app or its data, or otherwise deleted through available device or app controls.

Because normal camera frames are used for real-time movement recognition and are not intentionally saved as video by VolleyMotion, there is normally no stored camera video for us to retain or delete.

If you have a privacy or deletion question concerning information controlled by us, contact us using the email address below.

8. Permissions and Your Choices

You control camera permission through your device settings. You may revoke camera permission at any time, although motion-controlled gameplay will then be unavailable.

You may also uninstall the app or clear its local app data using your device's available controls.

9. Children's Privacy

VolleyMotion is not directed to children under 13 unless specifically stated otherwise in the applicable store listing. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information handled by the app. No electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-Party and Platform Services

VolleyMotion relies on operating-system and app-store services to provide features such as camera access and purchases. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Links opened from the app may also lead to third-party websites governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
