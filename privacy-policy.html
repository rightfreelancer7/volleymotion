VolleyMotion Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VolleyMotion (the "App") accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

1. Camera and Motion Recognition

VolleyMotion uses your device's front camera to recognize body movements used for gameplay, including swings, jumps, blocks, hand raises, and other motion-based actions.

Camera images and video frames are processed on your device during gameplay for real-time motion recognition.

VolleyMotion does not save, record, retain, or upload camera images, video recordings, or video frames.

Camera and motion information is used only to provide motion-controlled gameplay and related gameplay features.

You may disable camera permission at any time through your device settings. Camera-controlled gameplay will not function while camera access is disabled.

2. Gameplay and Activity Statistics

VolleyMotion may calculate and display gameplay and activity statistics based on gameplay duration and detected movements.

These statistics may include:

  • gameplay duration;
  • movement counts;
  • jump-related activity;
  • hand or swing activity;
  • match and gameplay statistics; and
  • other estimated gameplay or activity measurements.

Gameplay and activity statistics may include fitness-related estimates calculated from gameplay duration and detected activity.

These statistics are estimates provided for general gameplay, informational, and fitness purposes. They are not medical measurements and should not be considered medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

3. Local Storage

VolleyMotion may store certain information locally on your device to provide App functionality and remember your preferences.

Locally stored information may include:

  • gameplay and activity statistics;
  • game settings and preferences;
  • sound and coaching preferences;
  • onboarding preferences; and
  • other settings required for normal App functionality.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded by VolleyMotion to a developer-operated server or backend.

4. User Accounts

VolleyMotion does not require you to create a user account.

VolleyMotion does not maintain a user-account database containing usernames, passwords, profiles, or similar account information.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

VolleyMotion may offer subscriptions or purchases through the applicable app store provider.

Payment processing, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and subscription management are handled by the applicable app store provider.

VolleyMotion does not directly collect or store your full payment-card, bank-account, or app store account credentials.

The App may receive purchase or subscription status information necessary to provide or restore subscribed features.

Information handled independently by the applicable app store provider is subject to that provider's privacy practices and policies.

6. Information Sharing

VolleyMotion does not sell your personal information.

VolleyMotion does not share camera images, video frames, or locally stored gameplay and activity statistics for advertising purposes.

Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

7. Data Security

VolleyMotion minimizes data handling by processing camera and motion information on your device and storing applicable gameplay information locally.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the App. However, no device, electronic system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

VolleyMotion does not operate or use a developer-controlled server or backend for storing user data.

Camera images and video frames used for motion recognition are processed on your device during gameplay and are not saved or retained by VolleyMotion.

Gameplay and activity statistics, App settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Locally stored information may remain on your device until:

  • it is overwritten through normal App use;
  • you clear VolleyMotion's App data through your device settings;
  • you reset the information through an available App function; or
  • you uninstall VolleyMotion.

Information handled independently by an app store provider may be retained according to that provider's own policies and applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Deletion and Deletion Requests

VolleyMotion does not require users to create an account and does not operate a server or backend that stores user data.

Information stored by VolleyMotion is stored locally on your device.

You can delete locally stored VolleyMotion information by:

  • clearing the App's storage or data through your device settings;
  • using an available reset or deletion function within the App; or
  • uninstalling VolleyMotion from your device.

Camera images and video frames used for motion recognition are not saved or retained by VolleyMotion and therefore do not require a separate deletion process.

10. Health and Fitness Information

Gameplay and activity statistics displayed by VolleyMotion may include fitness-related estimates based on gameplay duration and detected movement.

These values are estimates intended for general informational and fitness purposes only.

VolleyMotion is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or medical monitoring.
