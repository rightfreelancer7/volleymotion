VolleyMotion Support

Help and support for VolleyMotion, the motion-based volleyball game that uses your iPhone camera to respond to supported body movements.

Getting Started

  • Place your iPhone upright on a stable surface or stand.
  • Use the front-facing camera and allow Camera access when requested.
  • Step back until your head, shoulders, arms, and both hands are clearly visible.
  • Follow the on-screen guide and voice cues during gameplay.

Camera or Tracking Issues

If the game cannot detect you correctly:

  • Make sure the camera lens is not covered.
  • Use the game in a well-lit area.
  • Keep your upper body and both hands inside the camera frame.
  • Move farther from the phone if the app asks you to step back.
  • If Camera permission was denied, enable it in iPhone Settings and reopen VolleyMotion.

Subscriptions and Purchases

VolleyMotion may offer auto-renewable Monthly and Yearly Premium subscriptions. Any introductory free trial, pricing, renewal date, and billing terms are shown by Apple before you confirm a purchase.

You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription from your Apple ID subscription settings. If you previously purchased a subscription and it is not recognized, use the app's Restore Purchases option.
